Account & data deletion

For the Marugen app by Marugen Fish Farm / Marugen Koi Farm.

Request deletion without the app

Email marugenfishfarmaiagent@gmail.com with the subject Marugen account deletion. Send from your account email if possible and include the account email you want deleted. If you cannot access it, explain this so we can verify ownership. You do not need to reinstall or sign in to the app.

Email an account deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, copy the email address above and send the request using any email service. Never send your password, verification codes or full payment card details.

Delete selected data instead

You can request deletion of selected personal data without closing the entire account. Email the same address with the subject Marugen data request and describe what you want removed.

In the app

Open Profile → Delete Account and review the confirmation. Saved addresses, wishlist items and your reviews can also be removed individually.

What is deleted and retained?

Account and shopping data is kept while needed to provide the service. You can request account and associated data deletion in Profile or by emailing our privacy contact without installing the app. After verifying the request, we remove sign-in access and personal account data, including saved addresses, wishlist items, reviews, profile photos and notification tokens. We retain only records needed for accounting, tax, delivery disputes, fraud prevention or other legal obligations. Order and payment records may include the order contents, amounts, transaction references and recipient or delivery details required as supporting business records. These are restricted to authorized staff and are not used to recreate your account. Singapore business records generally need to be retained for at least five years from the relevant Year of Assessment or GST accounting period. A legal hold or unresolved dispute may require longer retention. Contact us to ask which records apply to your request. Anonymous usage statistics may remain. Service-provider backups and crash records expire according to their retention schedules; data necessary for a legal obligation is not erased prematurely.

Timing and verification

We aim to respond to verified requests within 30 days. We may need information to verify account ownership, and will explain any required retention or delay. Deletion does not delete your Google or Apple account or cancel an existing order, refund or delivery obligation.

Read the Privacy Policy for the information we collect, service providers, retention and your choices.
