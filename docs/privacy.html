1. About this policy

Marugen Fish Farm (also known as Marugen Koi Farm) operates the Marugen shopping app and marugen.pages.dev website. This policy explains the personal data we handle when you browse, create an account, place orders, contact us or request deletion. Our farm is at 21 Neo Tiew Lane 1, Singapore 718788. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

2. Account and shopping information

We collect the name, email address, phone number and delivery addresses you provide; your account identifier and sign-in information; an optional profile photo; orders, product selections, order remarks, payment status and transaction references; saved wishlist items; ratings and reviews; and enquiries you send to us. Google or Apple sign-in provides the account identifier and profile information you authorize, such as your name, email and profile image. We do not receive your Google or Apple password. Reviews and their display names may be visible to other customers. Uploaded profile photos are stored at publicly accessible image URLs, so please do not upload sensitive documents.

3. Device, usage and website information

We record app visits and product views, including timestamps, product identifiers and your account identifier when signed in, to understand how the shop is used. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device notification token to deliver updates. Release versions use Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, diagnostics, app and operating system versions and installation identifiers. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process network information, including IP addresses and security logs. The app stores session and appearance preferences on your device. The website loads Google Fonts and may open external map or contact services; those services receive the network information necessary to handle your request.

4. Why we use data

We use this information to authenticate accounts, maintain your profile, process payments and orders, arrange delivery, respond to enquiries, show your order history, manage wishlists and reviews, send notifications, measure shop usage, diagnose failures, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Payments and service providers

Stripe processes payment details. We store transaction references and payment status, not full card numbers or card security codes. Supabase provides authentication, database and image storage. Google Firebase provides notifications and crash diagnostics. Google and Apple provide optional sign-in. EasyParcel and delivery partners receive the recipient name, phone number, delivery address and shipment information needed to deliver an order. Cloudflare hosts the website. Email, telephone, WhatsApp or map providers process information when you choose those contact options. We disclose only information needed for these services, and may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate fraud or protect safety.

6. Security and international processing

We use encrypted HTTPS connections, account authentication and database access controls to protect information. Access to customer records is limited to authorized shop staff and relevant service providers. Providers may process data outside Singapore. Their security, processing and retention practices also apply to the services they provide. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Never send passwords, verification codes or full payment card details in an enquiry or deletion request.

7. Retention and account deletion

Account and shopping data is kept while needed to provide the service. You can request account and associated data deletion in Profile or by emailing our privacy contact without installing the app. After verifying the request, we remove sign-in access and personal account data, including saved addresses, wishlist items, reviews, profile photos and notification tokens. We retain only records needed for accounting, tax, delivery disputes, fraud prevention or other legal obligations. Order and payment records may include the order contents, amounts, transaction references and recipient or delivery details required as supporting business records. These are restricted to authorized staff and are not used to recreate your account. Singapore business records generally need to be retained for at least five years from the relevant Year of Assessment or GST accounting period. A legal hold or unresolved dispute may require longer retention. Contact us to ask which records apply to your request. Anonymous usage statistics may remain. Service-provider backups and crash records expire according to their retention schedules; data necessary for a legal obligation is not erased prematurely.

8. Your choices and requests

You can update your profile and saved addresses in the app, remove wishlist items and your own reviews, and control notifications in app or device settings. You can ask us for access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Send requests from your account email when possible. If you cannot access it, contact us for identity verification. We aim to respond to verified privacy and deletion requests within 30 days and will explain any required retention or delay. You do not need to reinstall the app. Deleting your Marugen account does not delete your Google or Apple account or automatically cancel an existing order, refund or delivery obligation.

9. Children and changes

The shop is intended for adults making purchases, not children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate. We will publish updates to this policy with a revised date and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.

10. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, account deletion or deletion of selected data, email marugenfishfarmaiagent@gmail.com. Use the subject “Marugen account deletion” for an account request, or “Marugen data request” for another request. Include your account email and describe the request. We may ask for information needed to verify ownership, but never your password or verification code.
